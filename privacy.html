Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Purpose

DnD Obsidian Sync is a personal, non-commercial setup that uses Rclone to synchronize private Obsidian notes with Google Drive.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google Drive permissions to read, create, update, and delete files as needed for synchronization.

Data Storage

Files remain on the user's computer and in the user's Google Drive account. Rclone stores connection credentials locally on the computer.

Data Sharing

This personal setup does not operate a separate server that collects user data. The application owner does not sell or share Google user data with advertisers or data brokers.

Revoking Access

Google Drive access can be revoked at any time through Google Account security settings. Local Rclone configuration and credentials can also be removed from the computer.

Third-Party Services

Google Drive and Rclone are used for file synchronization and are subject to their respective terms and privacy practices.
